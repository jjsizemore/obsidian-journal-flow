# Daily Notes and Guided Journals

The language used for day-level notes and guided personal reflections.

## Language

**Daily Note**:
The general workspace and record for a particular calendar day.

**Check-In**:
A snapshot of how someone is doing, the emotions they are experiencing, and the factors affecting those feelings.
_Avoid_: Check-in, Check In

**Mood**:
The single overall answer to “How are you doing?”: Very bad, Bad, Okay, Good, or Great. It is distinct from the multiple emotions a person may experience at once.

**Practice Gratitude**:
A guided journal for reflecting on what someone is grateful for.
_Avoid_: Mindfulness as the name of this journal; mindfulness is a different practice.

**Analyze Thought**:
A guided journal for examining a particular thought and how it affects someone's feelings.
